01 / Software workflow

Software for the second layer.

The MicahMeds concept is designed to support 911 and EMS while software organizes qualified response pathways. Eligibility, authorization, and operating rules would be defined with appropriate clinical, legal, and agency partners.

01

Signal

A request can begin through the software experience or another authorized emergency-response workflow.

02

Context

Organize location, event context, and consented information for review.

03

Assess

Compare available response pathways when authorized information is available.

04

Coordinate

Support status visibility, escalation, and handoff design while 911 remains the emergency path.
