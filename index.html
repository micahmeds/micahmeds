Whensecondsmatter

MicahMeds is developing patent-pending software for the life-saving seconds when someone needs help and a qualified person nearby may not know an emergency is happening.

Emergency systems measure both ends of the wait. Almost nothing holds the middle.

Distance, traffic, call volume, staffing, and aging technology all accumulate in that stretch. So does qualified help that happens to be nearby and has no way of knowing it is needed.

It is a coordination problem before it is anything else — and coordination is a thing software can be built for, carefully.

Alongside 911. Never instead of it.

Our software organizes what is known during an emergency — what happened, where, what help is available and qualified, and how the event escalates and is handed off — so the people already responding can act on better information.

The underlying technology is patent-pending. Its design stays private, and detail is shared with partners under confidentiality.
