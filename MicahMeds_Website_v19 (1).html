The Problem

Help exists. It just cannot get there in time.

Our 911 system was built decades ago. It leaves communities waiting 7 to 14 minutes for help that — in cardiac arrest, stroke, overdose, or severe trauma — is needed within 4 to 6. In historically underserved neighborhoods, that wait runs longer still.

Nothing exists between calling 911 and waiting. There is no second tier to absorb the urgent calls that are correctly triaged behind the critical ones — so a fall, a diabetic emergency, or a stroke waits in the same queue.

The Mission

A supplement to 911 — not a replacement for it

Emergency medical services are resource-constrained by design, and urgent calls that are not immediately life-threatening are triaged behind those that are. Meanwhile, thousands of qualified people are already on the road — and no system connects them to the emergencies around them.

MicahMeds is that connection. The triage engine compares projected response times in real time and dispatches whichever qualified responder can arrive first, with EMS notified in parallel and retained as the default for critical calls.

Become a Responder

A workforce program, not a gig.

MicahMeds is building a training program for people who already drive — rideshare, delivery, and commercial drivers making a living behind the wheel — and turning that time on the road into a credential, a career, and a way to save lives in their own neighborhoods.

Responders operate as licensed independent contractors: they own their vehicles, set their own hours, and accept or decline each dispatch. All complete background screening and vehicle inspection, and carry minimum liability coverage, under medical director oversight and documented protocols.

🩺
Tier P · Credentialed Professionals

If you are already a nurse, physician, PA, or paramedic, you are the network.

Thousands of licensed clinicians drive rideshare and delivery between shifts, on days off, and throughout residency. They are already on the road, already trained to assess and stabilize, and frequently within blocks of an emergency they are qualified to manage — with no mechanism that permits them to respond.

MicahMeds is that mechanism. Off-duty clinicians respond within their existing scope of practice, with no additional training required — activating an enormous credentialed population that is already out there, and already qualified.

Tiers 1–3 · Certified Training Pathway

Already driving? We will train you.

No clinical background required. MicahMeds provides the certification pathway and the dispatch network to put it to use — so that the hours you already spend on the road become a credential that belongs to you, wherever you go next.

Start here

Tier 1 — NEMTAC

Forty hours of training plus First Aid and CPR. The entry point for any driver, with no prior medical experience needed.

Scope of response
Falls · Minor bleeding · Diabetic events · Panic episodes · Minor burns
Advance to

Tier 2 — EMT-Basic

120 to 150 hours to a nationally recognized EMT-Basic certification — a credential that opens doors across the entire healthcare field.

Scope of response
Cardiac arrest · Respiratory distress · Severe bleeding · Anaphylaxis · Overdose · Stroke · Severe trauma
Grow into

Tier 3 — EMR / Paramedic

1,000+ hours at EMR or Paramedic level. A full clinical career, reached one tier at a time. Opening as the network matures.

Scope of response
Advanced cardiac life support · Advanced airway · Complex trauma management
The ladder is the point. A driver can enter at Tier 1 with no medical background and progress toward a full clinical career — each credential nationally recognized and permanently theirs, whether they stay with MicahMeds or not.
